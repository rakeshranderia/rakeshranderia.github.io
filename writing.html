Writing & Notes

Useful ideas, when there is something worth saying.

Selected writing across data governance, AI governance, technology leadership, cybersecurity and practical IT operations. Longer articles live on the platform that best fits the subject; the website and GitHub remain the primary home for frameworks, tools and reference material.

Approach

Experience first. Content second.

I do not maintain a publishing schedule for its own sake. Most material starts with real delivery work, interview preparation, technical investigation or a practical problem worth structuring more clearly.

Where that work remains useful outside the immediate context, it may become an article, framework, playbook or interactive tool. This page is a curated index rather than a mirror of every article.
