Resources

Practical tools, not more shelfware.

Delivery Lifecycle · v1.0.0

Enterprise Hello World

A deliberately simple application demonstrating the full delivery lifecycle: requirements, Git, CI, Infrastructure as Code, testing, UAT, release, hypercare and BAU transition.

The application itself is intentionally trivial. The surrounding delivery controls, project workflow, defect simulation and operational evidence are the focus.

The live demonstration is hosted on a free service tier, so the first request after a period of inactivity may take up to approximately 60 seconds while the service starts.
