Resources

Practical tools, not more shelfware.

Practical tools for technology governance and decision-making. These resources are designed to be lightweight enough to use in real environments, adaptable to different organisations, and focused on helping teams make deliberate, proportionate and well-owned technology decisions.

AI Governance · v0.2

AI Use-Case Assessment

A practical pre-implementation assessment for evaluating an AI use case across business value, organisational data classification, DLP, platform risk, impact, human oversight, security and lifecycle governance.

The assessment is designed to help teams reach one of four clear outcomes: proceed, proceed with controls, undertake further assessment, or do not proceed.
