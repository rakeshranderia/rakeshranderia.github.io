Resources

Practical tools, not more shelfware.

Practical tools for technology governance, delivery and decision-making. These resources are designed to be lightweight enough to use in real environments, adaptable to different organisations, and focused on helping teams make deliberate, proportionate and well-owned technology decisions.

Available Resources

AI Governance · v0.2

AI Use-Case Assessment

A practical pre-implementation assessment for evaluating an AI use case across business value, organisational data classification, DLP, platform risk, impact, human oversight, security and lifecycle governance.

The assessment is designed to help teams reach one of four clear outcomes: proceed, proceed with controls, undertake further assessment, or do not proceed.

Delivery Lifecycle · v1.0.0

Enterprise Hello World

A deliberately simple application demonstrating the full delivery lifecycle: requirements, Git, CI, Infrastructure as Code, testing, UAT, release, hypercare and BAU transition.

The application itself is intentionally trivial. The surrounding delivery controls, project workflow, defect simulation and operational evidence are the focus.

The live demonstration is hosted on a free service tier, so the first request after a period of inactivity may take up to approximately 60 seconds while the service starts.
