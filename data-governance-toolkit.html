Data Governance Toolkit

Classify the data, understand its lineage, and govern it properly.

A connected practical toolkit for classifying data, understanding how it moves and changes, and embedding ownership, controls and evidence into normal delivery and operations.

Why these three pieces belong together

  • Classification tells us the control requirement.
  • Lineage tells us everywhere that requirement has to follow.
  • The operating model defines who owns the decisions, controls and evidence over time.
