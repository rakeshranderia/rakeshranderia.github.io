How I Work

Keep technology connected to business outcomes

The detail changes by organisation, but the leadership pattern is consistent: understand the problem, make trade-offs visible, deliver deliberately and measure the result.

Understand

3

4

Deliver & Secure

Programs, change, controls, vendors and transparent escalation.

Supportability, resilience, automation and continuous improvement.

6

Measure Value

Outcomes, adoption, risk reduction, service performance and learning.
